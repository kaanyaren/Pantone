Same-code counterparts (C ↔ U, TCX ↔ TPX) represent the same ink or dye number on different substrates, never interchangeable colours. CMYK values use the unprofiled device formula and are not Pantone Color Bridge ink builds. Thread RGBs are digital shade-card approximations; verify physical cones before production.
