what ΔE00 means

ΔE00 is the CIEDE2000 colour difference between two Lab values. It weights lightness, chroma and hue the way the eye does. Zero is identity; above five reads as a different colour. It is guidance, not a pass mark.

Colophon

Approximate colour reference. Screen colour is not ink, paper, or thread. Not affiliated with Pantone LLC, Madeira, or Durak.

Sources

Pantone C/U: Pantoner (MIT). TCX: pedroduke/pantone-tcx (ISC). TPX: color-books (legacy, unlicensed). Madeira: Ink/Stitch palettes (GPL-3.0). Durak: digital shade chart.

Method

sRGB rows, Lab D50, CIEDE2000 with kL = kC = kH = 1. CMYK uses the naive unprofiled formula and is not an ink build.
